ANSWER. Neither story from flights.csv holds here, and the data say why: the level of the series rose 3.5x (513 to 1,770) but the yearly swing stayed in the same band (428 in 2000, 502 in 2009; correlation of swing with level only 0.23), so swing as a share of the level FELL from 0.75 to 0.54. The additive residual's spread is the steadier one (late/early 1.03 against 1.32), so the seasonality is a roughly constant number of people, not a share of a growing total - the opposite of the airline-passenger result, and reported as it came out.
Caveat: the 2008-09 recession pushes the level up sharply in the last two years, so 'level' there is partly a one-off shock, not steady growth; this is one industry in one country.
