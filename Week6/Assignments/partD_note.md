# Part D1: interactive version of partB_2

`partD_interactive.html` rebuilds the Task B composition chart in Plotly (open it in any browser, it works offline).

**What the interaction lets a reader do that the static chart cannot:** hover any month to read solar MWh, total MWh and the solar share exactly, and drag the range slider to compare any two stretches of the 18 months, such as Sep 2025 against Sep 2026, instead of trusting the one comparison I chose to print.

**What it lets them do that I would rather they did not:** hide the grey "all other sources" series and zoom in, which makes solar's bars fill the whole chart and turns a share of 4 to 8 percent into something that looks like it dominates the grid, and it lets them pick the two months that flatter their argument.
